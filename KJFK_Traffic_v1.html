KJFK · New York Kennedy ATC Simulator

Four runways in two parallel pairs, 13L/31R with 13R/31L and 4L/22R with 4R/22L. Each configuration lands on one runway and departs from another. All traffic is airline IFR traffic.

OBJECTIVE

  • Arrivals (blue aircraft) enter on a real arrival: CAMRN from the south, PARCH from the east, PAWLN from the north, PUCKY from the northwest. After the last STAR fix New York Approach vectors them to the final. The sim keeps a default route to the IF so nobody gets lost, but direct-to and vectors give the real flow. Label text turns yellow once they are established on the localizer.
  • Departures (green aircraft) line up and follow a departure: SKORR off 31L, KENNEDY vectors off the others. The initial altitude is 5000 ft. Clear them to 12000 ft or above before their last fix, where they are handed off. Above 10000 ft they accelerate automatically to 320 kt. The STOP SPAWN button above the departure strips (or D) stops new departures.
  • Keep 3 NM / 1000 ft separation. Amber means predicted conflict, red means loss of separation. TCAS TA/RA alerts are modelled.
  • New York airspace is tight: LaGuardia, Newark and Teterboro traffic is not shown, but the departure and arrival gates are real, so departures often cross under arrivals.

CONTROLS

  • Mouse wheel zooms at the cursor. Drag pans. Click an aircraft or its strip to select it. Double-click centres it. Hover an aircraft for extra label info.
  • The panel at the bottom sets altitude, heading, speed, direct-to, holds, ILS clearance, go-around and take-off clearance. Pilots read back by voice (🔊 toggles).
  • Keys: ←→ heading ±5 (Shift ±10) · ↑↓ altitude ±1000 (Shift ±100) · WS speed ±10 · I clear ILS · G go around · H hold at present position · N resume nav / exit hold · T take-off · D stop / resume departure spawn · A stop / resume arrival spawn · L show / hide minor points · C centre · Space pause · 123 speed · Esc deselect
  • Headings and courses are true. Add 13° for the magnetic values on the charts: the 31R ILS reads 301 here and 314 on the chart.

ILS HOW-TO

  • The purple cone (±15°, 28 NM) is the localizer capture zone. Published fixes: the IF and the FAF on the extended centreline (31R CATOD and MALDE, 13L TELEX and CAXUN, 22L ROSLY and ZALPO, 4R ZETAL and EBBEE). Descend to about 3000 ft, fly a heading that crosses into the cone at 20–40° to the course, then press CLEARED ILS.
  • Aircraft that cannot capture the glideslope by 2 NM go around. A go-around climbs straight ahead to 4000 ft.
  • Below 10000 ft the speed limit is 250 kt. The transition altitude is 18000 ft and the base minimum altitude is 2000 ft.

MINIMUM ALTITUDES

  • The MVA layer shows illustrative sectors over the high ground of northern New Jersey, the Hudson Highlands, the Taconics and Connecticut. Unshaded airspace is 2000 ft. The altitude controls will not clear an aircraft below the sector it is in.

HOLDING

  • Pick a fix in the HOLD row: CAMRN, ROBER, PUCKY, DPK. Optionally change inbound course / turn direction, then press HOLD. HOLD PPOS holds at the present position. Any new heading or EXIT leaves the hold.
Entertainment simulator. Must not be used for real navigation. Runways come from FAA data. Fix coordinates come from FltPlan.com and navaids from FltPlan.com and the FAA charts. IF and FAF positions are computed from their published DME distances, and the IF altitudes for 13L, 22L, 22R, 31L and the FAF altitudes for 22L and 22R are estimates. STAR routes are from older CAMRN and PARCH charts, and the ROBER arrival, which the FAA has since cancelled, is left out; PAWLN and PUCKY are newer RNAV arrivals whose coding was not available, so their routes are approximate, and their version numbers may be outdated. Departure routing after the first fix is simplified to the exit gate. Holds are illustrative. Traffic from other New York airports is not modelled. Altitude constraints appear on the selected aircraft's route but are not flown automatically.
