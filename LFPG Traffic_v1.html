LFPG · Paris Charles de Gaulle ATC Simulator

Two independent runway pairs. North: 09L/27R (2700 m, arrivals) and 09R/27L (4200 m, departures). South: 08R/26L (2700 m, arrivals) and 08L/26R (4142 m, departures). All traffic is IFR.

OBJECTIVE

  • Arrivals (blue aircraft) enter on a real LFPG STAR and continue into its daytime RNAV initial approach. Traffic through LORNI and MOPAR lands on the north pair, traffic through OKIPA and BANOX on the south pair.
  • CDG initial approaches end in radar vectors. At the last point the crew calls for vectors: turn them onto the localizer, descend to about 5000 ft and clear the ILS.
  • Departures (green aircraft) appear lined up on an inner runway and follow a SID. Climb them to FL150 or above before the last fix of the SID, where they are handed off.
  • Keep 3 NM / 1000 ft separation. Amber means predicted conflict, red means loss of separation. TCAS TA/RA alerts are modelled. Approaches to the two pairs are independent, as the AIP allows, so aircraft established on different localizers do not trigger separation alerts.

CONTROLS

  • Mouse wheel zooms at the cursor. Drag pans. Click an aircraft or its strip to select it. Double-click centres it. Hover an aircraft for extra label info.
  • The panel at the bottom sets altitude, heading, speed, direct-to, holds, ILS clearance, landing runway, go-around and take-off clearance. Pilots read back your instructions by voice (🔊 toggles).
  • Keys: ←→ heading ±5 (Shift ±10) · ↑↓ altitude ±1000 (Shift ±100) · WS speed ±10 · I clear ILS · G go around · N resume navigation.
  • The compass (bottom-left) shows the selected aircraft's heading, cleared heading, its ILS course and the bearing to your cursor.

ILS HOW-TO

  • The purple cones (±15°, 28 NM) are the localizer capture zones. Published fixes on each extended centreline: the IF (IG27R, IG26L, IG09L, IG08R at 5000 ft) and the FAF, about 14 NM out.
  • Intercept at 5000 ft inside 18 NM with no more than 50° to the localizer. Below 10000 ft the speed limit is 250 kt.

HOLDING

  • Holds exist at LORNI, OKIPA, BANOX, MOPAR, ENORI, NANOP and ROMGO (patterns not drawn). Pick a fix in the HOLD row, change the inbound course or turn direction if you wish, then press HOLD.
Entertainment simulator, not for navigation. Runways, fixes, STARs, SIDs and initial approaches come from the eAIP France, AIRAC 29 OCT 2026.
