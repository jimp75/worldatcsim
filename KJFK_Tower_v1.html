Data
  • From FAA data via AirNav: runway ends, lengths, displaced thresholds, elevation, frequencies, published STAR and SID names.
  • From OpenStreetMap: the position of each terminal, used to place terminals around the ring.
  • From the IVAO KZNY SOP: runway configurations, ring direction (A clockwise, B counter-clockwise), airlines by terminal.
  • Reconstructed: all taxiway geometry. A and B and the K entry to 31L are real names. The other connector letters are placeholders, and their positions are not surveyed.
  • Placeholder gates: gate numbers and positions are invented, in the right order around the ring. Only the sizes are chosen by terminal role.
  • Estimated: STAR and SID fixes (placed by bearing only), final approach fixes, buildings. New Terminal 1 and Terminal 6 are not modelled.
  • For simulation only. Not for navigation.
