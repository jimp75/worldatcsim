LPPT · Lisboa Humberto Delgado ATC Simulator

One runway, 02/20 (3707 m, thresholds displaced 90 m and 499 m), used in mixed mode: arrivals and departures share it. Runway 17/35 is closed. Arrivals are sequenced by the Point Merge System onto PESEX (RWY 02) or UPKAT (RWY 20). All traffic is airline IFR traffic.

OBJECTIVE

  • Arrivals (blue aircraft) enter at the first fix of a real STAR. Each flies to an entry point of a point merge arc, then along the arc at a level altitude. Clear them direct to the merge point when the spacing is right, descend them to 4000 ft and then clear them for the ILS. Their label text turns yellow once they are established on the localizer.
  • Departures (green aircraft) line up on the same runway and follow a SID. Clear them to climb to FL150 or above before the last fix of their SID, where they are handed off. Above 10000 ft they accelerate automatically to 320 kt. The STOP SPAWN button above the departure strips (or D) stops new departures from appearing.
  • Keep 3 NM / 1000 ft separation. Amber means predicted conflict, red means loss of separation. TCAS TA/RA alerts are modelled: on an RA one aircraft climbs 1000 ft and the other descends 1000 ft.

SHARING THE RUNWAY

  • Space arrivals 6 NM at the threshold to leave a gap for a departure, or 3 NM to land two in a row.
  • A take-off is refused while traffic is landing, rolling, inside 2 NM on final, or going around nearby.
  • An arrival goes around automatically if an aircraft is still lined up when it reaches 2 NM, or if the runway is still occupied at 1 NM.
  • RWY 20 departures start from intersection U5 (2412 m available); heavies backtrack to full length at S4.

CONTROLS

  • Mouse wheel zooms at the cursor. Drag pans. Click an aircraft or its strip to select it. Double-click centres it. Hover an aircraft for extra label info.
  • The panel at the bottom sets altitude, heading, speed, direct-to, holds, ILS clearance, go-around and take-off clearance. Pilots read back your instructions by voice (🔊 toggles).
  • Keys: ←→ heading ±5 (Shift ±10) · ↑↓ altitude ±1000 (Shift ±100) · WS speed ±10 · I clear ILS · G go around · H hold at present position · N resume nav / exit hold · T take-off · D stop / resume departure spawn · A stop / resume arrival spawn · L show / hide PT points · C centre · Space pause · 123 speed · Esc deselect
  • The compass (bottom-left) shows the selected aircraft's heading, its cleared heading (green bug), the ILS course (purple) and the bearing to your cursor (yellow). Move the cursor on the map to read bearing and distance.
  • Headings and courses are true. Add 2° for the magnetic values on the charts: the ILS course reads 023/203 here and 025/205 on the chart.

ILS HOW-TO

  • The purple cone (±15°, 28 NM) is the localizer capture zone. Published fixes on the extended centreline: the IF and merge point (PESEX for RWY 02, UPKAT for RWY 20, 4000 ft, 180 kt, 11.3 NM) and the FAP (NETVO for RWY 02, TOPVI for RWY 20, 3000 ft, 8.2 NM). From the merge point the aircraft is already aligned, so press CLEARED ILS on the way there or fly a heading that crosses into the cone at 20–40° to the course.
  • Once on the localizer the aircraft follows a smooth intercept, slows along a distance-based speed profile, limits its descent rate while it is still fast, and captures the glideslope. Aircraft that cannot capture the glideslope by 2 NM go around. You can order a GO AROUND at any time on final: straight ahead, climbing to FL070.
  • Below 10000 ft the speed limit is 250 kt. The transition altitude is 4000 ft and the base minimum altitude is 3000 ft.

MINIMUM ALTITUDES

  • The MVA layer shows minimum-altitude sectors around the main high ground: Sintra, Arrábida, Montejunto, Aire and Candeeiros, Lousã and the eastern and southern ranges, with one altitude label each. Unshaded airspace is 3000 ft. The altitude controls will not clear an aircraft below the sector it is in. Move the cursor over the map to read the MVA at that point.

HOLDING

  • The published holds are in the HOLD row: MAZUK and ORTUG for RWY 02, EKLID and DEKKI for RWY 20, the merge points and the intermediate holds. Pick a fix, optionally change inbound course / turn direction, then press HOLD. The aircraft flies a direct, parallel or teardrop entry. HOLD PPOS holds at the present position. Any new heading or EXIT leaves the hold.
Entertainment simulator. Must not be used for real navigation. Runway, ILS, frequencies and holds come from AIP Portugal LPPT AD 2. STAR routes come from the coding tables in NAV Portugal's PMS optimisation report (October 2024), so their version numbers may differ from the current AIP; ESEBI, which is ATC request only, is left out. The current SID names and end fixes are real, but their coding tables could not be read: the first legs come from the 2022 AIP SIDs (PT412, PT416, MONUR, NETVO, GUDAV) and the routes between those and the end fixes are direct, so the SIDs are an approximation. Each SID is one variant: 1N or 1X on RWY 02, 1S on RWY 20. Altitude constraints appear on the selected aircraft's route but are not flown automatically. Minimum altitudes and the coastline are illustrative models.
